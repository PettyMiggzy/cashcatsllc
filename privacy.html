Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

This policy covers the CashCats LLC website (cashcatllc.help), the PFP Studio, the CashCats Swap, and the CashCats Telegram bot and Mini App. CashCats LLC is a community meme project; $CASHCATSLLC is a meme token with no intrinsic value. We keep data collection to the bare minimum needed for these tools to work.

The short version: we don't run user accounts, we don't sell data, we don't set tracking cookies, and we never receive your wallet's private keys. Most of what the tools do happens in your own browser or directly on a public blockchain.

1. What we receive, and why

  • Telegram Mini App / bot. When you open our Mini App inside Telegram, Telegram shares a signed initData blob that can include your Telegram user id, first name, username, profile photo and language, plus your IP address. We use your user id only at the moment you tap “Send my PFP to the chat,” so the bot can deliver that image to you. We validate that data and use it for that single action — we do not store it in a database.
  • Wallet / swap. The CashCats Swap runs entirely in your browser. When you connect a wallet, we see your public wallet address to build the transactions you review and sign yourself. We never receive or store your seed phrase or private keys. Transactions are broadcast by your wallet to the blockchain.
  • PFP Studio. Image generation and compositing happen in your browser. Images you make are not uploaded to us unless you choose the Telegram “send to chat” action described above.

2. On-chain data is public

Blockchain transactions — buys, sells, the 1% holder dividend, and airdrops — are recorded on the public Robinhood Chain and are visible to anyone via a block explorer. That is inherent to public blockchains and is not something we control or can delete.

3. Third-party services

Some features call third parties, each governed by their own privacy terms:

  • Telegram — delivers the bot and Mini App.
  • DexScreener — price data and token logos.
  • GoPlus — the optional token-safety scan in the swap.
  • Public RPC / block explorer providers — reading and broadcasting on-chain data.

Requests to these services may expose your IP address to them as a normal part of an internet request.

4. Data retention & your choices

We do not maintain a database of users, messages, or wallet activity beyond what is already public on-chain. Because we store little to nothing about you, there is generally nothing for us to delete — but if you believe we hold data about you, contact us via the Telegram community and we will address it. You can stop sharing data at any time by not using the Mini App, bot, or swap.

5. Children

These tools are not directed to anyone under the age required to use Telegram or to hold crypto assets in their jurisdiction. Do not use them if you are below that age.

6. Not affiliated; not financial advice

CashCats LLC is a parody community project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Robinhood Markets. Nothing here is financial advice. The 1% holder dividend is funded by voluntary routing fees and distributed by the team; it is not a promise of profit.

7. Changes & contact

We may update this policy; the “last updated” date will change when we do. Reach us through the CashCats LLC Telegram community.
