What this page cannot show you

Token movements emit an event and can be listed exactly. Payments of the chain's own gas coin do not emit anything, so they cannot be enumerated from a public node without scanning every block — they show up only as a fall in the balance above. If the wallet ever pays anyone in the native coin, put the transaction hash in spend.json and it will be listed here with everything else.
