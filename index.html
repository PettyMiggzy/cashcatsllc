No. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Robinhood Markets in any way. It's a meme built on a piece of public startup trivia — the name that reportedly came before the app. Fan fiction with a ticker.

How does the buy & burn work?

Trades routed through the CashCats Swap collect a small fee in ETH. That fee is used to market-buy $CASHCATSLLC and send it to the dead address, permanently removing it from supply. The token's own tax stays 0/0 — the fee lives in the swap layer, not the contract. Burn totals on this page are read live from the chain.
